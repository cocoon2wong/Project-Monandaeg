## Getting Started

*Project-Monandaeg* is a repository dedicated to posting model weights from our papers.
For some of our early papers, they may be posted in the `Releases` page of their repos.
Here, we have provided most of the model weights of our newly published papers.
The corresponding `Tensorboard` files are also provided.
You can use these weights to validate the results we report or to further validate your ideas.
Before doing so, you may need to prepare the dataset files.
Get started by clicking the button below.
